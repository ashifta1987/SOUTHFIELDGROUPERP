title: Receipts and payments
---

Record money in or out, choose cash or bank, and allocate it to the bills it settles. Anything left stays on account. Cash rules: a cash receipt of ₹2 lakh or more from one person in a day is refused; a cash payment over ₹10,000 to one person in a day needs a reason that Admin sees.
