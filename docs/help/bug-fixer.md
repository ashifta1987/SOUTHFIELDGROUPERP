title: Bug fixer
---

Every problem found in Tally loads and checks lands here: possible duplicates, Tally groups to map, missing GSTIN or HSN, bills that do not add up, vouchers changed in Tally after loading. Open an item, read what was found, and use the action it offers (merge, keep separate, accept change) or dismiss it with a note. Loaded Tally data is never edited.
