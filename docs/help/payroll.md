title: Payroll

Mark attendance (present, half day, leave, weekly off, overtime hours). Prepare payroll for a company and month: pay is worked out from attendance, overtime at twice the hourly rate, allowances and advance recovery. Someone other than the preparer approves it; then it posts to the books and staff get a 'payslip ready' message with no amounts.
