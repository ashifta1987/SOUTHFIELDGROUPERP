title: Making a tax invoice
---

Choose the company GSTIN, the customer and the date, then add lines. The server works out the GST: CGST + SGST inside the state, IGST to another state, at the rate of the HSN on the invoice date. Save a draft, check the preview, then Post. Posting takes the next invoice number, writes the books and queues the WhatsApp message. A posted invoice is never edited: issue a credit note or cancel it (which posts a reversal).
